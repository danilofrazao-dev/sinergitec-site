Getting Started

VoiceLink needs TWO separate sign-ins. It is the same Google account both times — Google is the door, not the account.

1 · Sign in to Sinergitec

Identifies you to VoiceLink. This is your product account — it carries your licence, trial and PRO status.

2 · Sign in to Groq

Powers the speech-to-text. A separate provider — use the same Google account. Never used Groq? Signing in creates your account automatically.

3 · Paste your API key

Create a free key in the Groq console, paste it into the Get API tab, and start talking. Your key stays on your machine.

Both are required. Signing in to one does not sign you in to the other — they are unrelated services. Without both, the START button will not run.
